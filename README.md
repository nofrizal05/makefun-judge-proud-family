# Make the AI Judge Feel Proud: A Family Scene

A short family scene for make.fun's "Make the AI Judge Feel Proud: A Family Scene"
bounty. The entry is a single warm, specific moment: a grandfather sitting by the
window each evening, quietly watching his nine-year-old granddaughter teach the
neighbour boy to read on the porch — passing on, word by word, the very kindness
he once taught her. He says nothing. He just smiles, proud that what he planted
is now growing in smaller hands.

## The entry

> My grandfather sits by the window each evening, tea going cold, watching my little sister teach the neighbour boy to read on the porch. She is nine, sounding out every word, as he taught her. He says nothing. He just smiles: the kindness he planted now grows in smaller hands.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network access) writes the scene
above to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`). It asserts the text
is between 1 and 280 characters and is not blank.

## Run it

```
python3 main.py
```
